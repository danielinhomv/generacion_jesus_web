The Gospel

This Is the Most Important
Thing You Will Ever Read

Every mission trip, every school visit, every Academy course, every chaplain trained — all of it exists for one reason: to bring people into a living relationship with Jesus Christ.

The Gospel is not complicated. But it is life-changing. Read carefully. We invite you to respond.

1

Step 1

Who Is Jesus?

Jesus Christ is not merely a teacher, a historical figure, or a moral example. He is fully God and fully man — the eternal Son of God who entered human history.

He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, performed miracles, and taught with authority. He is the fulfillment of centuries of Old Testament prophecy. He is Lord over all creation.

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God… And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us."

John 1:1, 14

"Jesus said to him, 'I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.'"

John 14:6
2

Step 2

Why We Need Salvation

The Bible teaches that every person — without exception — has sinned. Sin is not only outward behavior. It is an inward condition: a heart that chooses itself over God. And sin has a consequence.

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."

Romans 3:23

"For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

Romans 6:23

Sin separates us from God — now and for eternity. We cannot fix this on our own. No amount of good deeds, religious practice, or personal effort is sufficient. We need a Savior.

3

Step 3

The Cross

God did not leave us in our sin. He acted. Out of His love for humanity, God sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to take the punishment that our sin deserved.

Jesus was crucified — nailed to a Roman cross. He suffered and died. This was not an accident of history. This was the plan of God from eternity: that the sinless Son of God would bear the sin of the world so that those who believe in Him would not perish, but have eternal life.

"For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life."

John 3:16

"But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."

Romans 5:8

On the cross, Jesus took the full weight of sin — past, present, and future — for every person who will receive Him. The debt was paid. Justice was satisfied. Love was demonstrated.

4

Step 4

The Resurrection

Death could not hold Him.

Three days after His crucifixion, Jesus rose bodily from the dead. The tomb was empty. He appeared to His disciples, to hundreds of witnesses, and to those who had doubted. The resurrection is not a symbol. It is a historical event — and it changes everything.

"He is not here; He has risen, just as He said."

Matthew 28:6

"Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, and that He was buried, and that He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures."

1 Corinthians 15:3–4

Because Jesus rose from the dead, He has power over sin and death. His sacrifice was accepted. His victory is real. And because He lives, those who believe in Him will also live.

5

Step 5

Grace

Salvation is not earned. It cannot be earned. It is a gift.

Grace means that God freely offers forgiveness, new life, and eternal relationship — not because we deserve it, but because of who He is and what Jesus did. We cannot work for it. We cannot pay for it. We can only receive it.

"For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast."

Ephesians 2:8–9

This is what makes the Gospel "good news." There is nothing left for you to do except to receive what God has already done through Jesus Christ.

6

Step 6

Repentance and Faith

To receive this gift, two things are required: repentance and faith.

Repentance means turning. Not simply feeling sorry — though genuine sorrow for sin is real — but actually turning away from sin and turning toward God. It is a change of mind that leads to a change of direction.

Faith means trusting. Not merely believing facts about Jesus, but personally trusting Him — placing your confidence in Him as your Savior and Lord.

"Repent, and let every one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins; and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit."

Acts 2:38

"If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved."

Romans 10:9

Repentance and faith are not things you manufacture on your own. They are responses to God's grace, enabled by the Holy Spirit. As you reach out to God honestly, He meets you there.

7

Step 7

Receiving Jesus Christ

You can receive Jesus Christ right now. This is not a religious ritual. It is a sincere, personal response to God — an honest turning toward Him in repentance and faith.

If you are ready to receive Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior, you can pray something like this:

A Prayer of Salvation

Lord Jesus, I know that I am a sinner and that I need You. I believe that You died for my sins and that God raised You from the dead. I turn from my sins now and I place my trust in You. I receive You as my Lord and Savior. Come into my life. Forgive me. Make me new. I give my life to You.

Amen.

If you prayed this sincerely — or if you want to know more before you do — we want to connect with you. Reaching out to speak with a real person is one of the most important things you can do right now.

What Happens Next?

Receiving Jesus Christ is the beginning, not the end. Here is what the Bible says happens when someone truly comes to faith in Christ.

You Are Forgiven

Every sin — past, present, and future — is forgiven through the blood of Jesus Christ. The record of debt is canceled.

"As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us."

Psalm 103:12

You Are Made New

The Bible says that anyone who is in Christ is a new creation. Old things pass away. New things come.

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new."

2 Corinthians 5:17

The Holy Spirit Lives in You

God Himself, through His Holy Spirit, takes up residence in the life of every believer. He guides, comforts, convicts, and empowers you to live for Christ.

"Do you not know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God?"

1 Corinthians 6:19

You Are Called to Grow

Receiving Christ is not the finish line — it is the starting line. You are called to read God's Word, pray, join a community of believers, and grow as a disciple of Jesus.

"As newborn babes, desire the pure milk of the word, that you may grow thereby."

1 Peter 2:2

You Are Part of the Church

You are now part of the global Body of Christ. Find a local church where the Bible is taught and where you can worship, be discipled, and serve alongside other believers.

You Have a Mission

The Great Commission belongs to every follower of Jesus — including you. Tell someone what happened. Share the Gospel. The story continues through your life.

"Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations."

Matthew 28:19

We Want to Walk With You

Whether you just received Christ, are still considering it, or want to grow as a follower of Jesus, we are here. These two steps can make all the difference.

Pray With Someone

Talk with a real person who can pray with you, answer your questions, and help you take the next step.
