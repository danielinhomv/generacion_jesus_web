Why Stories Matter

The Complete Jesus Generation Picture

Stories are not anecdotes. They are evidence. A potential partner should leave understanding that this is an established international ministry with national leaders, a proven school-chaplain strategy, an expanding training platform, and opportunities where their resources and relationships can make a meaningful difference.

A potential mission participant should understand that they are not traveling overseas looking for somewhere to minister. They are joining an established ministry network where doors are already open and where they will personally be equipped to share the Gospel.

A potential Academy student should understand that this training comes from a ministry actively doing the work — providing biblical, practical, and professional preparation that helps people become more effective as chaplains, pastors, missionaries, or Christian leaders.

And everyone should understand:

  • Jesus Christ is the center.
  • School-age children are the mission field.
  • Chaplaincy provides access and presence.
  • Evangelism introduces people to Christ.
  • Discipleship produces followers.
  • The Academy equips leaders.
  • Missions mobilize believers.
  • Leadership multiplication carries the Gospel forward.
