Other Ways to Give

Beyond the Gift Form

Jesus Generation can receive gifts in several forms. Complex gifts should begin with an inquiry so we can direct your generosity appropriately and responsibly.

  • In-Kind Gifts Equipment, supplies, and other tangible resources.
  • Corporate & Foundation Giving Engage your business or foundation alongside the mission.
  • Real Estate & Property Gifts of real estate and property directed through approved processes.
  • Legacy Giving Include Jesus Generation in your estate planning.

Cryptocurrency and equities/securities gifts are also accepted. Contact us to begin the conversation.
