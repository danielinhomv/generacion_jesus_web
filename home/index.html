Why Jesus Generation Matters

Presence in the school. The Gospel to a generation.

School-age children gather every day in one of the most influential environments in their lives: the school. Jesus Generation has spent decades developing a ministry strategy that places trusted Christian leaders within those school communities.

School chaplaincy gives us something extraordinarily valuable: presence. Chaplains can build relationships with students, teachers, families, and communities over time. From that foundation come opportunities for evangelism, prayer, discipleship, pastoral care, family ministry, and connection with local churches.

The objective is not simply to place chaplains in schools.

The objective is to reach a generation for Jesus Christ.

Jesus Generation Academy

Equipping Those Who Reach the Next Generation

Jesus Generation Academy is the training and educational arm of the movement.

The Academy provides accessible Christian education in English and Spanish for:

  • Chaplains
  • Pastors
  • Missionaries
  • Ministry leaders
  • Emerging leaders
  • Churches
  • Mission participants
  • Believers seeking deeper biblical training

Training can include Jesus Generation courses as well as carefully selected resources from trusted Christian educational and ministry partners.

Major pathways include:

Chaplain Training & Certification

Pastoral & Theological Education

Leadership & Train-the-Trainer

Missions & Evangelism

Discipleship

The Academy allows Jesus Generation not only to reach people, but to equip them to reach others.

MISSION TRIPS

Go Where the Doors Are Open

Jesus Generation mission trips provide an unusual opportunity for direct Gospel ministry.

When participating, school communities know a mission team is coming, and students and faculty can organize in advance. A typical 20-person team can divide into pairs, allowing ten groups to minister at the same time.

  • Every participant shares.
  • Every participant gives testimony.
  • Every participant learns to present the Gospel.
  • Every participant has the opportunity to invite people to receive Jesus Christ.

This is not a model built around one featured preacher and nineteen spectators. The team carries the Gospel together.

GLOBAL IMPACT

See What God Is Doing Around the World

Jesus Generation grows out of more than three decades of international ministry.

Today the broader network includes approximately 50,000 chaplains and ministry workers, 41,000 schools, and 20+ nations.

But the significance is not ultimately found in numbers. It is found in children hearing the Gospel, families receiving ministry, pastors being strengthened, chaplains being equipped, and national Christian leaders taking responsibility for reaching their own communities.

Each nation has its own story, leadership, opportunities, and testimonies of lives being changed through the Gospel.
