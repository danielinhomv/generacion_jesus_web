Privacy Policy

Jesus Generation protects your privacy.

Our promise

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information for another organization’s fundraising, advertising, or marketing.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide through subscriptions, inquiries, and donations to communicate with you, fulfill your requests, and administer ministry activities. Our website may also collect technical information and use cookies. Third-party payment services and embedded features operate under their own privacy policies.

Who can access it

Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to perform these functions. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect someone’s safety.

Safeguards and retention

We use reasonable security safeguards and retain information only as needed for ministry purposes and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from ministry emails at any time or contact info@JesusGeneration.global to request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Children

This website is intended for adults. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will identify changes through the effective date above.

Questions about your information? Write to info@JesusGeneration.global.
